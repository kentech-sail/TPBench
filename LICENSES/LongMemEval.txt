LongMemEval-KU (knowledge-update split, derivative of LongMemEval-S)

Source: https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval (LongMemEval-S release).

(per the LICENSE file in the upstream LongMemEval repository).
License URL: https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT

The LongMemEval-KU subset shipped here consists of probe rows built from the
72 knowledge-update questions in LongMemEval-S that pass the support/boundary
filter described in the paper.

Use within this artifact:
- Probe rows derived from LongMemEval-S carry session ids, gold answers, and
  prompts containing rendered turns. They are redistributed under the
  upstream MIT license terms.
- Aggregate JSON files contain only summary statistics.

Privacy note: LongMemEval-S is a public personal-memory benchmark. The KU
subset contains synthetic-style personal-memory turns. No additional
human-subject data is collected by this artifact. Reviewers and users should
defer to upstream policy for treatment of any personally-styled content.
